# स्मृतिकौस्तुभः

<div class="sk-noxlit">

A digital edition of the **Smṛti-kaustubha** of Anantadeva (Nirṇaya Sāgara Press, 2nd ed., 1931).
Pick a script from the menu at the top; the text is stored in Devanāgarī and transliterated in your browser.

**Phase 0 pilot:** only the Caitra- and Vaiśākha-kṛtyam (printed pp.85–117) are here so far. It has had a machine
cleanup but **no proof-reading against the page images yet**. Words marked <span class="sk-corr">like this</span>
are corrections of the OCR (hover to see what the OCR read); `[?]` marks a reading someone must check on the page.
