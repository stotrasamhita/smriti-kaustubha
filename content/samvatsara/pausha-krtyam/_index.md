<div class="sk-noxlit">

Pauṣa-kṛtyam, printed pp.432–439. It begins "अथ पौषकृत्यं" on p.432, after the Mārgaśīrṣa closing verses, and
ends "इति पौषकृत्यानि" on p.439, where the Māgha-kṛtyam begins. Most of it is the aṣṭakā rite: the pūrvedyuḥ-śrāddha,
the aṣṭakā and the anvaṣṭakya. The text places the first aṣṭakā on "पौषकृष्णाष्टमी", the dark aṣṭamī after the
Mārgaśīrṣa full moon (pūrṇimānta reckoning). As with the Bhairava-jayantī, these days are tagged in amānta terms:
tithis 22–24 of month 9.
