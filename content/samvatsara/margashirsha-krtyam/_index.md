<div class="sk-noxlit">

Mārgaśīrṣa-kṛtyam, printed pp.427–432. It begins "अथ मार्गशीर्षकृत्यं" on p.427, after the Kārttika closing verses,
and ends "इति मार्गशीर्षकृत्यम्" on p.432, where the Pauṣa-kṛtyam begins. The Bhairava-jayantī falls on the dark
aṣṭamī that the text calls both "कार्तिकस्यासिताष्टमी" and "मार्गशीर्षासिताष्टमी" (amānta and pūrṇimānta reckoning);
it is tagged as tithi 23 of month 8.
