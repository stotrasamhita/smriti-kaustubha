<div class="sk-noxlit">

Kārttika-kṛtyam, printed pp.358–427. It begins "अथ कार्तिककृत्यम्" on p.358, after the Āśvina closing verses,
and ends "इति कार्तिकमासकृत्यम्" on p.427, where the Mārgaśīrṣa-kṛtyam begins. The month is reckoned from the
Āśvina full moon here: pp.361–373 (running head आश्विनमासः) cover the dark fortnight, amānta Āśvina, up to
"इत्याश्विनमासः । अथ कार्तिकः" on p.373, and are tagged as tithis of month 7.
