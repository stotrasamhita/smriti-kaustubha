<div class="sk-noxlit">

Phālguna-kṛtyam, printed pp.513–519. It begins "अथ फाल्गुनकृत्यं" on p.513, after the Māgha closing verses, and ends
"इति फाल्गुनकृत्यम्" on p.519. The Holikā falls on the full moon (tithi 15) and the Dolotsava on the following
pratipad, which the text keeps with Phālguna (tithi 16).
