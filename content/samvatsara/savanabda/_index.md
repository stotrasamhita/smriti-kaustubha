The civil (sāvana) day, month and year, printed pp.554–557: weekday śrāddhas, oil baths, the Saturday vow against
Śani's affliction, Sunday vows. It begins "अथ सावनमासकृत्यम्" on p.554 and ends "इति सावनाब्दकृत्यम्" on p.557,
where the Bārhaspatya-year chapter begins. Its closing verse reads "सौराब्ददीधितिरियं" in the OCR. No month or tithi
tags.
