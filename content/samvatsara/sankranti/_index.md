The solar year and the twelve saṅkrāntis, printed pp.530–548 (running head द्वादशसौरसंक्रान्तिकृत्यं). It begins
"अथ सौरसंवत्सरकृत्यम्" on p.530, after the cāndrābda closing verses, and ends with the closing verse
"संक्रान्तिदीधितिरियं" on p.548, where the saura-year chapter begins. No month or tithi tags.
