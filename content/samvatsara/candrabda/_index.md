<div class="sk-noxlit">

The short chapter on the lunar year, printed pp.529–530: the sixty years Prabhava to Kṣaya, the five-year yuga
(saṃvatsara to idvatsara) and their gifts. It begins "उक्तचान्द्रसंवत्सरस्यावान्तरभेदानाह" on p.529, after the
adhimāsa closing verses, and ends "इति चान्द्रसंवत्सरकृत्यम्" on p.530. No month or tithi tags.
