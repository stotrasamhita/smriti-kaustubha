<div class="sk-noxlit">

The closing chapter of the Saṃvatsara-dīdhiti, printed pp.567–580: "अथोक्तवत्सरसमुदायरूपकलिसंबन्धिकार्याकार्यविवेकः",
what is to be done and what is forbidden (kali-varjya) in the Kali age, and when the Lord's name may be uttered.
It ends with the closing verses of the whole dīdhiti on p.580; the Āśauca-dīdhiti follows. No month or tithi tags.
