The Jupiter (bārhaspatya) year, printed pp.557–560 (running heads बार्हस्पत्याब्दकृत्यम्, सिंहस्थनिषेधादपवादः): Jupiter
in Leo and Virgo, what is forbidden while Jupiter is in Leo and the exceptions, and the setting, infancy and old age
of Jupiter and Venus. It begins "अथ बार्हस्पत्याब्दकृत्यम्" on p.557 and ends "इति बार्हस्पत्यवत्सरकृत्यम्" on p.560,
where the nākṣatra-year chapter begins. No month or tithi tags.
