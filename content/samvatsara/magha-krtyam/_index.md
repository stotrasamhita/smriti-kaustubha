Māgha-kṛtyam, printed pp.439–513. It begins "अथ माघमासकृत्यम्" on p.439, after the Pauṣa closing verses, and ends
"इति माघकृत्यम्" on p.513, where the Phālguna-kṛtyam begins. The Śivarātri topics (pp.481–512, "माघकृष्णचतुर्दशी",
"माघान्ते बहुले पक्षे") are tagged amānta Māgha kṛṣṇa 14, month 11 tithi 29; the amāvāsyā śrāddha is tithi 30.
