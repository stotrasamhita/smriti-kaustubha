<div class="sk-noxlit">

Āśvina-kṛtyam, printed pp.287–357. It begins "अथाश्विनकृत्यम्" on p.287, after the Bhādrapada closing verses,
and ends with the closing verses and "इत्याश्विनकृत्यम्" on p.357; the Kārttika-kṛtyam begins on p.358.
The running heads of pp.361–371 still read "आश्विनमासः" (the dark fortnight, amānta Āśvina), but the text
places those days in the Kārttika-kṛtyam, so they belong to that section.
