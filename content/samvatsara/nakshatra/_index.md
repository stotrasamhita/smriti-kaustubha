<div class="sk-noxlit">

The nakṣatra year with the yogas and karaṇas, printed pp.560–566 (running heads नाक्षत्रवत्सरकृत्यम्,
व्यतीपातव्रतम्, विष्टिनिर्णयः): nakṣatra śrāddhas and vratas and how to choose the day, the yoga vrata and the
Vyatīpāta, and the karaṇas with the Viṣṭi (bhadrā). It begins "अथ नक्षत्रवत्सरकृत्यम्" on p.560 and ends with the
closing verse "नाक्षत्रदीधितिरियं" on p.566. No month or tithi tags.
